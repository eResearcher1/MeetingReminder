Welcome to MeetMinder

Introduction

MeetMinder is your ultimate tool for creating and keeping track of meetings. Whether it's a professional meeting, a casual catch-up, or a reminder for important events, MeetMinder helps you stay organized and on time.

Features of MeetMinder

  • Set, track, and manage your meetings effortlessly.
  • Receive timely reminders 10 minutes before a meeting starts.
  • Easy-to-use interface with intuitive controls for managing meetings.

Benefits of Using MeetMinder

MeetMinder ensures that you never miss a meeting or appointment. It enhances your productivity by keeping you on track with your schedules, reminding you of upcoming meetings, and providing a seamless way to manage your time effectively. With MeetMinder, you can:

  • Stay punctual with reliable notifications.
  • Improve time management by easily organizing meetings.
  • Avoid last-minute rushes with timely alerts.

Required Permissions

1. Notification Permissions

MeetMinder requires permission to send notifications to alert you about upcoming meetings. On opening the app the first time, these permissions will be requested as shown below.

If permission was not granted the first time, when you click on plus button at the bottom right to add a meeting, the notification permission will be requested again. Select allow and thats all. Once allowed or denied, this request will not pop up again. Note that if denied, then you cannot add a meeting cause the app will not be able to alert you.

If you initially deny the notification permission, you can manually enable it by following these steps:

  1. Go to Settings on your device.
  2. Select Apps or App Management.
  3. Find and tap on MeetMinder.
  4. Select Notifications and toggle the permission to Allow.

Alternatively:

  1. You can also do this: Right click on the app icon on the mobile screen and select App Info. Then look for Notifications and toggle the permission to Allow

2. Exact Alarm Permission

MeetMinder uses exact alarms to ensure timely notifications about your meetings.

3. Disabling Battery Optimization

To allow MeetMinder to run efficiently in the background, you need to disable battery optimization for the app.

Follow these steps:

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Go to Battery or Power Management.
  3. Select Battery Optimization.
  4. Find MeetMinder and set it to Don't Optimize.

Alternative Method:

You can also adjust these settings by:

  1. Right-clicking on the MeetMinder app icon.
  2. Selecting App Info.
  3. Going to Permissions or Battery Optimization to enable the necessary permissions.

How to Add a Meeting

Adding meetings in MeetMinder is simple and straightforward:

  1. Tap on the Plus (+) Button at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Fill in the meeting details, such as title, date, time, and location.
  3. Once done, tap Save to add the meeting to your schedule.
